Privacy Policy

OpenBot Gmail is a personal application used only by its owner to access the owner's own Gmail account.

  • It requests Gmail access (read, modify, and basic settings) solely to triage, label, draft and forward the owner's own email.
  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
